Artificial Intelligence (AI) is the simulation of human intelligence in machines.
Machine Learning (ML) is a subset of AI that enables systems to learn from data.
Deep Learning is a subset of Machine Learning that uses neural networks with multiple layers.

LangChain is a framework used to build applications powered by large language models (LLMs).
It helps developers create chatbots, question-answering systems, and AI agents.

Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) combines vector search with LLMs to provide more accurate and context-aware responses.
Vector databases like FAISS and Chroma are commonly used in RAG systems.
